Classification and Fragmentation Analysis of Ordinary Chondrites from the Atacama Desert

Samanta Aravena1, Daniel Moncada1, Alfonso Mohor1, Diego Castañeda1, Roberto Valles1, Víctor Cárcamo1, Lorena Olivares1, Sebastián Gatica1, Matías Peña2, María José Figueroa Zambrano2, Grace Batalla3,4, Axel Escobar3,4, Lucas Cieza3,4

1Departamento de Geología, Universidad de Chile  ·  2Escuela de Geología, Universidad Mayor  ·  3Instituto de Estudios Astrofísicos, UDP  ·  4Cosmic Dust Laboratory, UDP

Abstract
We present a comprehensive classification and fragmentation analysis of 59 meteorite specimens recovered from the Atacama Desert (Catalina, El Médano, Los Vientos, and San Juan areas), forming the Beatriz Levi Repository at the Department of Geology, Universidad de Chile. The specimens were classified using magnetic susceptibility (KLY5-A Kappabridge), infrared spectroscopy (FT-IR), Raman spectroscopy, and petrography. Of the 59 specimens, 44 yielded magnetic susceptibility data, revealing 28 H-type, 12 L-type, 13 LL-type, 2 carbonaceous chondrites, and 4 unclassified. Fragmentation analysis based on corrected magnetic susceptibility (Δχ ≤ 0.04) identified 6 distinct falls in Catalina and 1 in Médano. Our results demonstrate that magnetic susceptibility, when combined with IR spectroscopy and petrography, provides a robust, cost-effective classification pipeline.
